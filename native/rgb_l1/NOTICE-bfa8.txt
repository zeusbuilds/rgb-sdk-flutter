SDK integration and recovery boundary
-------------------------------------
Backup capability statements apply to the Flutter RgbLibWallet API and its
platform operation policy. Calling bundled UniFFI/Rust mutation entry points
outside that policy can bypass restored-wallet safeguards. Restored-wallet
spending and mnemonic-only RGB recovery require an approved freshness and
cross-copy authority. Generic native refresh rejects restored snapshots,
because refreshing pending transfers may send acknowledgements or publish
transactions. See doc/RGB_LIB_BACKUP_RECOVERY.md for the supported contract.
